About

The College of Engineering Chengannur(CEC), was established by the Government of Kerala under IHRD in the year 1993. It has been approved by All India Council for Technical Education(AICTE) and affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University(KTU).

  • Electronics Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • General Engineering
