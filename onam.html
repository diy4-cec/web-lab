ONAM-THE NATIONAL FESTIVAL OF KERALA

About Onam

Onam, the national festival of Keralam, is an annual harvest and Hindu cultural celebrated mostly by the people of Keralam and is traditionally associated with the legend of the benevolent Asura King Mahabali, who once ruled Keralam, returning each year to visit his people.It is the official festival of the state and includes a spectrum of cultural events.

Celebrations

  • Pookkalam: Intricate and colorful floral carpets are laid out at the entrance of homes to welcome King Mahabali
  • Onasadya: A massive, multi-course vegetarian feast served traditionally on a fresh banana leaf, featuring up to 26 dishes and ending with payasam
  • Vallam Kali: Thrilling snake boat races held on the rivers of Kerala, drawing huge crowds of spectators
  • Traditional Attire: People wear new clothes; women frequently wear the classic white and gold Kasavu sari, while men wear mundu
  • Cultural Performances: Folk dances like Thiruvathirakali and energetic street performances like Pulikali (tiger dance) are core highlights
