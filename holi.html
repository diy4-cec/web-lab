HOLI-THE CELEBRATION OF COLOURS

About Holi

Holi is a sacred ancient tradition of Hindus. It is also observed broadly on the Indian subcontinent.The festival has many purposes; most prominently, it celebrates the beginning of spring. In 17th century literature, it was identified as a festival that celebrated agriculture, commemorated good spring harvests, and the fertile land.Hindus believe it is a time to begin enjoying spring's abundant colours and say farewell to winter.

Celebrations

  • Holika Dahan: People light large bonfires on the night before the main festival to symbolize the burning of the demon Holika and the victory of good
  • Rangwali Holi: The next day, crowds gather in the streets to smear bright colored powders, known as gulal, and spray colored water using water guns
  • Families enjoy traditional sweets like gujia and a cold milk beverage called thandai
